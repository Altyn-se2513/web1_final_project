WHY WE EXIST

Stories make room for new perspectives.

Digital Book Collection brings book discovery, personal organisation and online reading together in one simple experience.

It is designed for students and casual readers who want an approachable way to explore literature, remember titles they love and build a steady reading habit.

The collection combines a browsable catalogue with a personal bookshelf stored in the browser. Public-domain texts can be explored through the reader, while external book recommendations help visitors discover titles beyond the local selection.

This project is a front-end learning demonstration. Account information is stored locally in the current browser and is not sent to a server.
